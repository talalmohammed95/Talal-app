Put Peninsula on your home screen

One tap to open it, like an app. It takes 20 seconds.

On your iPhone

  1. Tap Open Peninsula below. It opens in Safari.
  2. Tap Share (the square with an arrow). On newer iPhones, tap ••• first.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

On your Android phone

  1. Tap Install Peninsula below, if you see it. Or tap Open Peninsula.
  2. In Chrome, tap ⋮ at the top right.
  3. Tap Add to home screen (or Install app), then Install.
